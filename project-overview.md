# Coffee O'Clock Cafe Management System

## Project idea

Coffee O'Clock is a PHP and MySQL web application for organizing cafe
operations. This folder contains the second staged update: staff account
access, menu records, and basic inventory and stock-purchase tracking.

## Update 2 scope

- Staff sign-in and sign-out, retained from Update 1.
- Add, list, pause, enable, and delete menu items.
- Track inventory names, units, current quantity, and reorder levels.
- Record purchases to increase stock and save quantity, unit cost, and date.
- Show summary counts for menu items, inventory records, low stock, and
  out-of-stock items.

## Not included yet

- Daily sales or sale-item records.
- Automatic stock deduction when an item is sold.
- Customer counts or visit tracking.
- Daily cost and profit reports.
- Staff roles or multiple cafe locations.

## Planned progression

1. Staff account access.
2. Menu and inventory management (this update).
3. Daily sales, customer counts, and costs.
4. Combined dashboard, testing, and final presentation.

## Technology

- HTML and CSS for the web pages.
- PHP for server-side requests, sessions, and validation.
- MySQL with PDO prepared statements for application data.

## Demonstration

Sign in, add a menu item and an inventory item, show the stock status, record
a purchase, and show that the available quantity has increased. Explain that
sales and customer tracking are planned for a later update.
